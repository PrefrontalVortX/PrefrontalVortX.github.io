Feel like saying hi, sharing a meaningful story, venting, or just letting your fingers stumble across the letters on your keyboard and create literal nonsense for the sake of a good time? Feel free to comment below.

One small request:

If you decide to comment something legible, I only ask that you please be kind and speak to your own experience. Unsolicited advice is not appropriate, as we cannot possibly know what’s best for anyone but ourselves.

It makes sense to feel anger or resentment. Those are human emotions. Please do not direct them toward any specific person or group.

Rejection, judgment, and criticism hurt. We know this. Let’s do something different here.

Optional. You can remain anonymous, share your real first name, or be whoever you want!
